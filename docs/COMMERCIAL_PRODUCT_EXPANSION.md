## Architecture, isolation and delivery
- One codebase supports hosted multi-tenant SaaS and separate self-hosted customer installations. The separately deployed VenueDesk Central Control Center manages customers, products/editions, license issuance and renewal, install/domain binding, subscriptions, operational support and update delivery; no access to tenant financial/customer secrets merely because of a support/license link.
- Central platform signs license grants using protected server private keys. Installations verify offline using public key, protected issuance timestamps and periodic central revalidation with a documented outage window; never trust client-only clocks or browser fields. Bind deployment UUID, business, approved domain(s), edition, max halls/users and entitled modules; server public IP is an additional corroborating signal with authorized rebind/migration.
- Customer-initiated activation/renewal requests contain structured nonsecret install identity, domain, public server address and desired entitlements plus nonce/timestamp and fingerprint. A hash alone is NOT a decodable request. Delivery may be automated or verified manual upload.
- Send persistent owner expiry reminder starting 15 days before expiration. Default seven-day backend grace; centrally signed separately configurable second extension. At the license expiry instant, the PUBLIC WEBSITE switches to branded Coming Soon / Under Maintenance and blocks public enquiries, new holds, merchant checkout and public payment callbacks that would create new bookings, EVEN during ordinary backend grace, unless separately signed public-operation extension. The public never sees license prompts. Preserve historical data, owner renewal/export access and pending payment reconciliation even after final grace; no deletion. One installation license covers the website and admin; no separate website key.
- Tenant, hall and role boundaries are enforced in backend policy, SQL joins, jobs, exports and lookup. Never treat a hidden menu or client-provided company ID as authorization. No inter-customer ledger or inventory access.

## Public enquiry and held-booking experience
- Public Check a Date takes exact numeric guest count (not a range selector), date, morning/noon/evening program and conditional hall field. Price is calculated from owner-defined nonoverlapping per-hall guest-count brackets, capacity, approved current price/rules and add-ons, then frozen into a versioned enquiry/quote snapshot.
- After submission, the same form moves to a summary screen with a unique enquiry ID. Unpaid enquiry does not block inventory/slot. Optional customer choice to pay a disclosed 5% deposit/hold (credited toward total; clear expiry/refund policy) moves to a method screen then approved payment request/QR. Claim is PostgreSQL-concurrency protected and confirmation requires independently verified funds, not customer-entered reference alone. Prevent double-booking, repeated callbacks, late payments and incorrect refunds.
- If no supported and configured online payment method is enabled, offer enquiries but hide the paid hold checkout.

## Payments, funds, expense and approvals
- Per-client optional modes independently for JazzCash, Easypaisa and Raast: OFF, manual QR/account transfer, authorized merchant API, or both when technically supported. Optional approved unified gateway can handle multiple payment rails while preserving actual method. Raast is bank/wallet transfer rail, not independent cash wallet. No scraping or reuse of website browser login/session cookies as merchant integration. Merchant APIs may be unavailable to personal accounts; show only verified supported capabilities.
- Provider account numbers/QR and credentials are tenant specific, protected, masked and owner configured, with multiple bank and wallet accounts; capture account holder/bank/branch/account/IBAN/currency and opening balances. External reference is distinct from internal unique transaction/receipt ID. Manual QR proof and typed reference are NOT independent verification: owner/authorized staff reconcile against received funds before verified status. Official signed callbacks/webhooks require replay protection, idempotency and reconciliation; receiving APIs do not imply payout rights.
- Extend the EXISTING invoice/payment/refund and exactly balanced journals rather than a parallel ledger. Preserve historical cash/bank receipts; customer deposits are liabilities until earned and account transfers are not income. Vendor bills/payables, employees permanent/temporary wages/advances/overtime, utility bills, purchases, reimbursable staff spend, multi-bank deposits, checks, withdrawals and owner/staff custody flows require source/destination/receipt/status/audit.
- Every cash handover/payment yields unique numbered voucher/receipt and BOTH parties' signatures/evidence. Track physical cash even if evidence pending; do not claim machine vision has validated a signature. Inter-person transfers require independent receiver confirmation and discrepancy handling. Owner sets per-manager per-transaction/day/week/category/hall/method limits (default deny until configured), exception approvals, owner post-review, no self-approval, and split-payment protections. Actions never silently delete history.
- Owner reports: event profitability, recognized income vs collections, deposit liabilities, cash/bank/wallet balances, receivables/payables, wages, vendors, utilities, daily/weekly/monthly/quarterly/yearly earnings, hall occupancy, discounts, refunds, audit and exports. Enforce report category/hall permissions server side.

## Hall inventory — ownership, audits and borrowing
- Stock ALWAYS belongs to an individual HALL, not pooled at owner/company level. Business owner may read aggregate reports, but balance and source of truth are per-hall per-item. Catalog per hall includes chairs, tables, plates, spoons, glasses, jugs, consumables, decor and serialized high-value assets. Owner/authorized manager can create item from inventory screen AND inline within multi-item borrowing form. Creating a catalog entry alone does not increase owned stock.
- Maintain immutable hall-scoped stock movements: opening inventory, purchase/addition with vendor/receipt, consumption, damage, owner-reviewed loss, repair, stocktake adjustment and loan dispatch/receipt/return. Distinguish owned-on-hand, borrowed-in, loaned-out, in-transit, damaged/unusable, reserved for event, and physically present. No double count of borrowed units as purchased/owned stock.
- Allow owner-defined post-event (selective high-loss items), monthly, quarterly and ad hoc stocktakes. Manager records physical quantities, reason, photo, condition and counting actor. Compare each item against expected physical on-premises count NET of all open loans and transfers, record discrepancies as unresolved first; authorized review closes adjustments. Immutable before/after evidence, time, actor and approver; never erase a count.
- Multi-line internal hall-to-hall and external establishment loans. Single loan can list different items (chairs, tables, spoons, plates, glasses, jugs etc) and quantities; dynamic Add Row, choose existing or inline create new item. For outside parties manually capture establishment, address, contact person, phone, purpose, expected return, borrowing FROM or lending TO; optional repeat partner directory; no other venue customer data exposure.
- Every loan records source/destination hall or external party, event, item and row quantities, dispatched vs received count and condition, who released and who accepted, signatures/evidence, return due date, partial return batches, each return line's counted accepted/damaged/lost units and discrepancy history. Source and destination acknowledge independently. Verified receipt may be signed by external party when no VenueDesk account; simply ticking a box is not independent acceptance.
- Automatic matched loan closure ONLY when EVERY line has returned the original quantity in acceptable condition, all required independent confirmations/documentation exist, and there are no unresolved losses/damage/money obligations; then generate numbered Borrowing & Return Completion Receipt/PDF with full rows and actors, immutable audit and report access. Otherwise partial/overdue/disputed and owner-authorized adjustment workflow, never silently destroy loan.
- Reports by hall/item/partner/event/date: opening stock + purchases + approved losses + transfer/loan adjustments = owned closing; physical/available vs borrowed totals, audit variance, new stock (e.g. 100 spoons), missing 20 and confirmed current total 180, damage, overdue loans, inventory value, replenishment minimum stock alerts. External loan fees/deposits use existing journal/approval services, not silent stock valuation.
- Lock hall+item rows in deterministic order during transfers/loans/adjustments; database constraints, duplicate request keys, tenant FK protections and concurrent-stock tests prevent double lending. Feature off until full scope authorization and accuracy gates verified.
