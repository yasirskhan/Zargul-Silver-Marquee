## 1. FINAL OWNER DECISIONS (do not revert to earlier 5-minute or manual-only model)
1. Single vendor portal host: **https://lichall.hafzatech.com** for HAFZATECH staff, customer self-service and customer installation API, with **separate protected routes** on the same host. Illustrative paths: `/staff`, `/customer`, `/api/v1` and `/help`. HTTPS required; actual DNS, certificate, VPS and accessibility are UNVERIFIED and must be provisioned.
2. VenueDesk ships **vendor-managed trusted central base URL** and trusted public signature-verification keys in its official installer. Customer does **not** type vendor IP/domain. Client uses DNS and validated HTTPS, outbound only. Bind one authenticated installation identity/keypair to its customer/license. Do not follow untrusted redirects or let an ordinary customer point license verification at a fake host. Support authenticated **signed endpoint migration** and exceptional offline signed recovery if HAFZATECH must move its domain.
3. One license covers public venue website + VenueDesk dashboard for that installation/business, not two separate fees. Supports 6-month or 1-year terms, plans, enabled modules, max halls/staff and approved domains, identity and edition. Reissued signed revisions retain the same **license ID**, while extending expiry and authorized features; archived versions remain auditable.
4. **AUTOMATIC ONLINE LICENSE STATUS VERIFICATION EVERY 15 DAYS**, not every five minutes. Also a **user-triggered Settings → Licensing → Verify License** anytime. Normal requests verify the locally cached digital signature and validity **without calling central**. Central verification updates the signed license when an approved, fully paid renewal is ready. The owner sees last verification, next scheduled check, verified/local/offline state, expiry and failures. If the 15-day scheduled check fails, retry with bounded exponential backoff and idempotency; a network failure is not revocation and must never invent a license renewal.
5. Owners receive in-app expiry reminders beginning **15 days before license expiry**. The locally verified date governs when offline. An early paid annual renewal extends from existing expiry (preserve remaining paid days); likewise approved 6-month renewal. Billing Officer must independently confirm settlement before license issuance/renewal. License Officer can issue only eligible paid grants, with central authorized signing service.
6. At the signed subscription expiry instant, **public website shows generic branded Under Maintenance / Coming Soon**, with approved hall contacts but **no license/renewal notices**. Public new enquiries, new holds and new checkout/booking-init operations are blocked; continue lawful payment reconciliation/refunds for previously initiated transactions through restricted authenticated services. Owner/admin management operational **grace is seven days** as previously approved, then limited renewal, support, reports/export and historical business access. No data purge, no silent financial/history deletion. Extra grace requires separately approved **signed** extension with dates/scope; only explicitly granted public-operation extension may keep public intake live.
7. The owner accepts that **without check-ins a self-hosted installation may not detect central revocation before its next successful online verification or signed expiry**. The regular cadence is 15 days; a strict 72-hour offline cutoff is **NOT owner-approved**. Document tradeoff clearly rather than silently introducing earlier 5-minute polls or 72-hour blocks.

## 2. CENTRAL PORTAL DEPLOYMENT AND DATABASE BOUNDARY
Vendor portal at `lichall.hafzatech.com` with logical modules and separate auth/security for staff, customer billing portal and installations. A single domain does not mean shared staff/customer sessions or unrestricted API routes. HAFZATECH-controlled dedicated server/VPS or managed platform with HTTPS, domain/registrar MFA, DNS recovery, firewall/WAF/rate limits, PostgreSQL, background jobs/queue, segregated app secrets, object storage, monitored backups and restore tests. Central signing private keys must be isolated to a signing service/KMS/HSM equivalent; never include vendor signing secret in customer code, ordinary controller env, support accounts or exported backups without controlled encryption.
Canonical data relations:
`vendor_customer → customer_contacts → subscriptions → invoices → verified_payments → licenses → registered_installations → signed_license_revisions`.
Separately `central_staff_users → roles/permissions/delegations → approval_decisions/audit` and `customer → tickets → replies/status/read receipts/attachments`.
Preserve one support/billing/customer relationship across multiple separately licensed installations. No customer's hall finances, bookings, payroll, private leads or tenant bank-account credentials are replicated into vendor central by default.

## 3. VEN UEDESK CLIENT BOOTSTRAP & TRUST
Official installation package contains trusted `VENUE_CENTRAL_URL=https://lichall.hafzatech.com` and key identifiers/public keys; app obtains server endpoint using DNS at runtime (NOT hard-coded public IP). Installer generates unique installation identifier plus local protected signing/encryption keypairs. Activation performs authenticated enrollment/owner verification/domain proof, device nonce/timestamp, signed structured activation request (human-readable *after authorized decryption*), and stores a locally signed trusted entitlement. Encrypt any sensitive activation payload for central and optionally encrypt license delivery for the intended installation using audited HPKE/AEAD or a vetted protocol. TLS is mandatory; digital signature proves authenticity/integrity, encryption ensures confidentiality, and they serve **different** functions.
Use standard cryptography (e.g., Ed25519 signatures and vetted encryption, key identifiers/rotation), versioned canonical format and explicit algorithms. Validate complete license: issuer, product, installation/customer binding, authorized domains, time validity, monotonic revision, features/hall/staff limits, unique serial, verified signature; fail closed on forged/signed-by-unknown/future/mismatched/replayed grants. Do NOT assert that license contents can be permanently hidden from root/admin of self-hosted machine. Encrypt signing keys/installation private material at rest; use OS secrets store permissions where applicable; secure recovery/transfer.
Customer's Licensing page: License ID, edition/limits, paid-through and current signed expiry, status, 15-day warning, last *central* verified time, next 15-day check, error reason, `Verify License` action and `Request Renewal` / `Contact Support`. Never say central verified if only local cryptography was checked. Click Verify authenticates installation and downloads a newer approved signed revision only if available; preserve old valid license if response fails authenticity. Source of truth for paid invoice = central billing ledger.
**Domain/server migration:** owner submits a request, proves new domain, HAFZATECH authorizes rebind/recovery with MFA/approval, original install superseded, replacement keypair and revision signed. Staging/dev instances separately licensed/isolated from production payments and bookings. Use approved domain(s), installation ID, optional IP metadata—not dynamic IP as sole license lock.

## 4. LICENSE SCHEDULE & SECURITY ACCEPTANCE
- Routine local verification on license-relevant operations/page loads: verify cached signature/time/binding without HAFZATECH network dependency.
- Automatic central authentication/check-in every **15 calendar days**; rate-limited manual check anytime; successful replies update last-central-verification timestamp. Safe retry after failures, queue and alert on sustained failures; don't mistake a missed poll for license revocation.
- Security revocation/suspension and entitled feature changes delivered at the next successful central check-in/manual Verify (not push to a customer firewalled VPS). A self-hosted admin who deliberately disconnects may continue until signed validity ends; do not promise instant remote shutdown. Revocation semantics, legal customer notice and incident appeals recorded.
- Signed versioned policy updates (including designer-credit position/enablement) may be downloaded at check-in. **Urgent security announcements and support replies** must have a separately agreed faster, authenticated synchronization channel if needed; DO NOT silently change license check frequency. Support messages can sync more often without revalidating license.
- Renewal must not change plan, term or validity before independent verified settlement + authorized licensing action; use transactions, idempotency keys, exact invoice/payment/license identifiers, immutable audit. Different paid-through date vs license expiry must be shown and reconciled.
- Server unavailability/outage, clock rollback, stale snapshot, revoked installation credentials, recovered corrupt files, key rotation and lost VPS/domain must have explicit integration tests and fail-safe recovery.

## 5. STAFF ACCOUNTS, BILLING AND APPROVAL MATRIX
Central HAFZATECH staff users are not client owner/manager accounts. Authenticate MFA, preferably phishing-resistant MFA for sensitive central roles; session/device alerts, revocation, no retrievable plaintext passwords. Role templates with **server-side granular permissions**, hall/customer scoping as needed; deny by default; assignment only by admin with no self-elevation.
- **Super Administrator:** controlled security recovery, privilege governance and high-risk decisions; never bypass immutable audit.
- **Staff Administrator:** create/reset/disable central staff, assign only authorized roles, cannot elevate self to Super Administrator or authorize personal payment/license requests.
- **Billing Officer:** draft/issue invoices, independently reconcile actual bank/wallet/provider settlement, record payment/refund/credit; cannot issue licenses, grant grace or mark own exception approved.
- **License Officer:** create paid activation and standard 6m/12m renewal after independently verified invoice; cannot self-verify payments or approve exceptional free grace.
- **License Supervisor:** approve exceptional grace with max length and reason; suspension/revocation and license transfer under policy; dual control on high-risk exception.
- **Plan Administrator:** version plans/price/features, cannot silently modify historical purchased entitlements or verify own sale.
- **Support Agent:** view licensed product, last status, plan and expiry where authorized; tickets/status/replies/knowledge, no finance secrets or license modification.
- **Support Supervisor:** support agent assignments, SLAs/escalations, approve reusable sanitized ticket solutions, not automatically a billing/license authority.
- **Auditor:** read-only permitted immutable audit and reports.
Define object + action scopes (`license.read_status`, `license.issue_paid`, `license.renew_paid`, `license.grace.request`, `license.grace.approve`, `license.revoke`, `billing.invoice.issue`, `billing.payment.verify`, `billing.provider.manage`, `staff.manage_roles`, `support.reply`, etc.). Owner-approved defaults forbid self-approval and require two authorized people for exceptional free extensions or emergency revocation. Audit before/after, actor, source, reason, timestamp, approval and recipient.

## 6. SEPARATE HAFZATECH PAYMENT PROVIDER DIRECTORY
Do **not** confuse vendor subscription billing with venue's own booking-payment methods. Central staff configures multiple vendor receiving accounts/providers: Pakistan domestic bank transfer, Easypaisa approved collection, international bank transfer/IBAN/SWIFT, Western Union where commercial purpose legally eligible, **PayPal later** if supported/eligible. Avoid claiming personal wallets expose commercial APIs. Each provider account: owner/title, country, receiving currency list, local/IBAN/SWIFT/reference, settlement verification mode, instructions, effective dates, account active/inactive, risk tier and permitted customers/plans/countries. Sensitive access encrypted/masked. Map allowed provider accounts **per client** and optionally per invoice, effective dates; client sees ONLY currently eligible HAFZATECH receiving instructions for their invoice currency/country.
Central invoice: customer, plan/term, currency, issue/due date, taxes, unique invoice number, line snapshots, permitted account/instructions snapshot, status, receipt/refund and verified payment references. **Uploaded screenshot/reference is only claimed evidence, NOT settlement**. Bank/Easypaisa/Western Union manual review requires independent actual funds; approved merchant API/PayPal future callback must be authenticated and idempotent, amounts/merchant/currency/capture verified. Suspicious bank account instruction changes require dual approval, notify affected customers, preserve already issued instructions and versioning to fight payment-redirection fraud. Track partial/overpayments, chargebacks, exchange rates and fees; no license on unpaid invoice. HAFZATECH seller entity/tax identity/legal invoices must be configured before live billing. The customer VenueDesk may independently configure its OWN JazzCash/Easypaisa/Raast/other gateway for wedding deposits, completely separate from vendor billing.

## 7. CLIENT INSTALLATION WIZARD: CURRENCY, TIME ZONE, BUSINESS RULES
One-time resumable, authenticated, safely closed after completion. Steps: installation/activation → owner with MFA → business/legal contacts/country → halls/address/phone/map/location/timezone → **operating currency** and **IANA business timezone** → 12/24-hour, language/date/week-start → three programs, guest tiers/pricing/grace/addons → website theme/footer/branding → tenant OWN banks/wallets/payments and optional 5% temporary hold → staff permissions/manager spend and audit → inventory optional item list/opening evidence → review/activate. Existing install migrations must preserve data and skip destructive reset.
- Support maintained ISO 4217 currency catalog incl PKR, INR, USD, GBP, AED and others; correct variable currency minor-unit precision (including 0/3 decimal). Exact decimal/integer minor units, never floats.
- Company default IANA timezone and optional hall-level override. DB actual timestamps in UTC; render, schedule events/cash-close/payroll/reporting in **business/hall local time**, never VPS timezone. DST/historical IANA rules; lock calendar local date, program and timezone to prevent ambiguous shifts.
- Venue operating currency is independent of subscription INVOICE currency payable to HAFZATECH. Issued invoices retain original currency and tax/exchange snapshots. Base currency change after financial transactions requires a governed migration, not a trivial dropdown change.
- Per-hall ownership of inventory stays unchanged; manager item-creation/stocktake and internal/external multi-item loan draft exist under default OFF flags, but witnessed handover, actual stock movements and numbered fully matched return completion receipts are still pending.

## 8. SUPPORT AND COMMUNICATIONS CONNECTION

Customer tickets saved to reliable local outbox with stable unique IDs; central server acknowledges real ticket number. Customer receives agent replies into local authenticated inbox, unread bell/banner persists until actually read; support available after license expiry, even if public site is under maintenance. External notification optional; no SSH/db credentials needed for ordinary support.
Different and optionally faster support sync (e.g. 1–5 min when connected) is independent of 15-day license sync. Preserve replay/idempotency/cursor and offline sync recovery. Diagnostics/attachments require customer authorization, scanning, retention, encryption, no automatic exposure of tenant financial/PII/private customer tickets. Smart Support opens ticket immediately and asynchronously suggests 1–3 vendor-approved matching manuals/videos or sanitized prior cases, **never auto-closes** and never leaks cross-client details. Central News, Help Library, role/version scoped announcements, developer-credit policy and release information sync through authenticated signed/validated messages. Software updates only signed, compatible release packages with backups/controlled migrations; no arbitrary central execution on customer VPS.
